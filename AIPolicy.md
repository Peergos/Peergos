# Peergos AI Policy

We are not religiously for, or against, AI assisted coding. AI is a tool just like any other. However, we do require that all code whether written by humans or AI must be reviewed and independently tested by a human. This is especially important for security critical software like Peergos. We have seen grave errors in AI generated code, but also critical bugs in human written code found by AI. Ultimately, as a human you are responsible for your code. 

We do not accept low effort AI contributions. Indeed, we don't accept any PR without first discussing the need and design with the maintainers, ideally in an issue.
